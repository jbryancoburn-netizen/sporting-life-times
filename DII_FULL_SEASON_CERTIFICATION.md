# Division II basketball: full-season school schedule certification

**Active project milestone (November 2, 2026): 100% of eligible NCAA Division II men's and women's programs independently certified for the full 2026–27 season.**

## Why this replaces the fixture-count milestone

The existing published ledgers have 3,264 men's and 3,790 women's games, with zero eligible schools at zero games. This does **not** mean all schools have their complete schedules. Each institution must have its OWN dated official 2026–27 calendar cross-reconciled against the published ledger. Do not use the old 1–6-game queue or a threshold like 25 fixtures as evidence of completion.

The canonical live certification register is generated by **the existing** `scripts/audit_d2_publication.py` in **`data/d2-publishing-audit.json`**. Inspect:

- `men.full_school_schedule_certification` and `women.full_school_schedule_certification`.
- `eligible_schools`, `certified_schools`, `uncertified_schools`, `stage_counts`, and every record under `schools[]`.
- Each school's `official_source_urls`, `official_source_rows`, `matched_source_games`, `missing_source_games`, `role_conflicts`, `tipoff_disagreements`, `outside_snapshot_examples`, `attestation_errors`, and `next_action`.

The audit's overall `status: PASS` means publication integrity passed. It does **not** mean schedules are complete; the separate `school_schedule_certified` value must be true for the relevant gender.

## Definition of CERTIFIED

A school is **CERTIFIED** only when ALL conditions have direct evidence:

1. Its eligible 2026–27 DII membership has been verified. Membership-pending/non-DII institutions are **excluded**, not manufactured into the completion denominator.
2. A complete official university 2026–27 dated fixture snapshot, **not** a short set of featured or conference-only games, is captured under `data/d2-official-school-grids-2026-27.json` in `schools[]`. Preserve the literal published opponent names and dates.
3. Every dated university game is classified as DII-vs-DII, another-division/exhibition, or unresolved/postseason TBD. Do not put unverified other-division teams into DII Elo. Do not silently drop nonconference games or tournaments.
4. Every eligible DII opponent on that snapshot maps to one unambiguous registered NCAA identity. The published game must match exact date, participants and home/away/neutral role. Preserve NCAA game ID and confirmed finals. Review tipoff discrepancies; clocks must be Eastern.
5. Every game in the school's DII ledger appears on its reviewed complete snapshot. Games added from independent NCAA data must also be accounted for; no one-sided phantom additions.
6. An **independent** official university/conference source checks the calendar, captured with a different domain from the primary university source. Any conflict or unresolved TBD is held, not guessed.
7. A reviewer records a dated per-school attestation with all mandatory fields listed below. `reviewed_by` is for audit accountability, never an inferred reviewer or fabricated signature.

## Explicit per-school attestation

The existing official school grid JSON includes `certification_assertions: []`. A genuine completed check may append one record in this shape:

```json
{
  "gender": "men",
  "owner_id": "<exact NCAA school ID>",
  "source_url": "https://<same official primary URL from schools[]>",
  "independent_source_url": "https://<different verified official domain>",
  "reviewed_by": "<person who actually checked all games>",
  "checked_on": "2026-11-02",
  "snapshot_rows": 28,
  "complete_calendar": true,
  "all_eligible_games_checked": true,
  "other_division_games_reviewed": true,
  "independent_crosscheck_completed": true,
  "unresolved_tbd_count": 0,
  "other_division_classifications": [
    {
      "date": "2026-11-21",
      "opponent": "<literal opponent name from the university snapshot>",
      "classification": "not_dii_2026_27",
      "membership_source_url": "https://<verified separate NCAA/membership evidence>"
    }
  ]
}
```

**Do not add this record until the check has actually been performed.** If the complete school calendar contains no other-division games, use `other_division_classifications: []`. Otherwise enumerate **every** non-DII/exhibition source row by exact date and original opponent, plus a reliable independent classification URL; unresolved identities cannot be certified. The audit rejects wrong-season/duplicate attestations, unverified second sources, unresolved opponents, mismatched snapshot counts, missing or extra DII games, conflicting home/away, and conflicting published clocks. The mere presence of an official URL, 25 games or a reciprocal conference schedule is insufficient.

## Durable official date-by-date capture in the existing publisher

The existing DII gender-ledger publisher now retains the actual dated, verified
rows from its **20 pinned university SIDEARM 2026–27 sources** (19 men, 1 women),
not just transient fetched counts. Each source is validated by season, gender,
university header, dates, and pinned campus time zone. The record is embedded in
`data/d2-{men,women}-2026-27.json` at
`quality.official_school_grid_recovery.live_calendar_captures[]`, which the
**unchanged** existing GitHub Actions workflow commits. Each record has
`capture_scope: "partial_verified_dated_rows"`, source URL, gender,
institution ID and its exact dated fixtures. A successful new collection
refreshes the source; a timeout retains the last correctly identified school
snapshot; lean hourly passes retain the last capture. Neither route fabricates
a final result or overwrites a canonical NCAA event ID.

The existing `scripts/audit_d2_publication.py` now reads those persisted
captures and compares each dated university DII match to the published
canonical ledger under `full_school_schedule_certification.schools[]`.
An automatic extraction is **never** treated as a complete season: tournament
TBDs, exhibitions, source row bounds, opponent classifications, and independent
review still require separate human/source verification. The audit reports
`eligible_with_auto_captured_unreviewed_dates`, missing mismatches, and
`PARTIAL_SOURCE_CAPTURE`. The `certification_assertions[]` registry remains
empty until an entire university calendar is independently verified.

The six newest priority-source URLs: Minnesota–Duluth (UMD), Roberts Wesleyan,
Queens (NY), Molloy, Mercy and Fresno Pacific men's basketball. Both official
academic-year URL spellings, `2026-27` and `2026-2027`, are supported with
strict sport/season checks. These are verified official site URLs, not certified

## Verified cross-date source conflict: Saint Martin's vs Hawaii Pacific

The 2026-27 Saint Martin's official university calendar shows its men's home
game against Hawaii Pacific on **December 19, 2026 at 7:00 PM Pacific
(10:00 PM Eastern)**. The existing NCAA canonical contest `6665372` lists
that pair on **December 20 at 12:30 AM Eastern**. These are contradictory
source dates/tipoffs for one named matchup, not grounds to publish two games.
The exact discrepancy is retained in
`data/d2-official-school-grids-2026-27.json:cross_date_quarantines[]`.
Only when the recorded NCAA ID, team pair, official URL and adjacent dates
all match may the existing DII importer remove a *duplicate synthetic*
`d2schoolgrid-2026-...` scheduled record; it never deletes NCAA games,
verified finals, unrelated repeated pairings, or broadcasts. The source row
remains visible to the certification auditor and the date/time conflict
remains **unresolved** until an independent verification establishes the
correct scheduled time. This protects both the ledger and the 1500 Elo replay.
