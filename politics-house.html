Read these colors correctly

State colors indicate the actual 2024 presidential two-party vote margin, not a House forecast. District colors indicate a dated 2026 House poll lead where available; otherwise, an actual 2024 House result only when boundaries have not changed. Missing comparable evidence stays neutral gray. Lighter colors mean narrower observed margins, not lower confidence or fewer votes.

The source rule

We use official state candidate-list snapshots, FEC district counts and registration counts, MEDSL's original-source-derived 2024 election returns, original published district-specific polling, and clearly labeled secondary summaries of published district polls. Samples and sponsors from the secondary summaries have not been independently verified. A registration does not prove ballot status; national generic ballot polls do not establish a local lead. Nine redrawn states cannot use 2024 district votes without reaggregation into 2026 boundaries. Our independent 2026 House model has not been certified.
