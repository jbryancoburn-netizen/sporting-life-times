Editorial standards

Attribution. We link to original reporting and identify the publisher for syndicated or feed-derived items.

Accuracy. Results, standings and schedules must come from a traceable source. Unverified data is labelled or withheld.

Transparency. Feed failures and stale updates should be visible to maintainers rather than silently presented as current.

Independence. Analysis and opinion should be distinguishable from reported facts.
