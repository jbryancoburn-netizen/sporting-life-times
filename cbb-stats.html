How the rankings work

Search and conference filters preserve national ranks. Ties share a rank at the precision shown. Player ranks use the selected games minimum. FG% and true shooting require 3 field-goal attempts per game, 3P% requires 1.5 three-point attempts per game, and FT% requires 1.5 free-throw attempts per game. These are Register thresholds. True shooting uses points divided by twice the sum of field-goal attempts and 0.44 times free-throw attempts. Lower points allowed per game ranks higher. RETRO uses the site’s published ratings and ranks.
