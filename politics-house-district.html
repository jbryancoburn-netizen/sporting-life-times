Sources and limitations

House district IDs and FEC registrations come from the original Federal Election Commission 2026 candidate master. Campaign registration is not a certified ballot listing. Historical U.S. House returns come from MIT Election Data and Science Lab (MEDSL) as preserved in the newspaper's electiondata.dev-derived original-source dataset. The 2024 presidential statewide comparison derives from MEDSL county returns; it is not a district or 2026 result. Candidate names, where present, are linked to the specific state election-office list. Any 2026 poll is individually linked to its original published release and discloses sample and sponsorship. No DDHQ probabilities, generic-ballot polling, partisan voting index estimates or made-up margins are described as original 2026 district polls.
