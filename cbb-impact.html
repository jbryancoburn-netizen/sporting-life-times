How Impact is calculated

Scoring credit is points minus 0.85 per field-goal attempt and 0.374 per free-throw attempt. Add 1.05 per offensive rebound, 0.35 per defensive rebound, 1.25 per assist, 1.65 per steal and 0.90 per block. Subtract 1.65 per turnover. Expand a single-game score to see its calculation. The Daily Ledger’s experimental shot-quality breakdown also shows verified heave exemptions when play-by-play reconciles with the box score. This register uses box-score Impact.

Season averages and games led use covered appearances. Exact ties count for each leader. The low-shot check counts appearances with five or fewer field-goal attempts and at least five positive Impact points from rebounding, assists, steals and blocks. A tied third place counts in the top three.
