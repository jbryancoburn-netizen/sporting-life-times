Projected Scores & Likely Spreads

Independent pregame forecasts for scheduled NBA games. Projected scores and point margins come from the verified possession-efficiency model; win percentages come from RETRO team-only Elo with the existing 40-point home-court adjustment. These are RETRO projections, not sportsbook lines.

Reading published NBA scoring inputs and upcoming games…

Reading the spread: “BOS −4.2” means RETRO projects Boston to win by 4.2 points. A projected score is rounded to whole points, so its displayed difference may not equal the decimal margin. A negative spread is not a wager recommendation.

Model boundaries: Injuries, confirmed lineups, travel and fatigue are not assigned untested point adjustments. Forecasts are provisional, especially early in the season. Games without validated team inputs are omitted rather than filled with invented projections.

Sporting Life & Times · NBA forecasts derive from verified 2026–27 NBA team box scores, sourced 2025–26 priors, frozen historical scoring calibration, and separately maintained RETRO Elo ratings. Current probabilities and spread estimates are different models; disagreement is displayed rather than concealed.
