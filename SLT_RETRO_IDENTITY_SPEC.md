# SLT retro broadcast rebuild / phase 1

Official wordmark: SPORTING LIFE AND TIMES (no leading The, no italic letters). Globe presents North and South America with Europe. Master palette is navy, royal blue, gold, cream and silver. Sport colors: crimson basketball, navy/silver football, forest-green baseball, royal-blue soccer, charcoal hockey. No golf.

The cream two-row scorebug is the default; full team-color rows are an optional feature. Full uniform colors require a verified uniformColor and uniformVerified=true. White uniforms render in a white row with contrasting outline. Never infer a game uniform from ordinary school colors. Timeout and challenge dots represent remaining counts, and missing counts show a dash. Bonus only appears when provided by verified data. Possession indicator is attached to the correct team.

Assets slt-retro-identity.js and slt-retro-identity.css only alter opted-in masthead links and explicitly rendered SLT scorebugs. The data engines, standings, Elo models and scheduled update workflows remain untouched.
