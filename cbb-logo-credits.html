Historical Logo Credits

Our school crests feature authentic historical artwork within the familiar double rings. The catalog below credits 273 archived marks sourced from Sports Logo History and SportsLogos.Net.

School names identify today’s programs; dates describe when each selected design was used. Earlier identities and alternate marks are included. Where older artwork could not be verified, some schools use explicitly dated early-2000s legacy designs.
