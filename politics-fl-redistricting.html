2026 FLORIDA CONGRESSIONAL REDISTRICTING

New lines, same district numbers. Different geography.

We compared the official Florida Legislature's P000C0109 (used for the 2024 election) with EOGPCRP2026 (used for the November 2026 election).

Both original files list the 2020 Census blocks assigned to each numbered congressional district. The analysis matches each block's old and new district and identifies changes in geographic assignment.

What this graphic does not show

These are unweighted counts of Census blocks. One block may contain many people or none. They are not population percentages, electoral swings, voting precincts, election returns or race probabilities.

No 2024 presidential votes have been retabulated by our model on the 2026 district boundaries.
