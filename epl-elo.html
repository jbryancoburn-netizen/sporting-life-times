How Premier League Elo is calculated Our independent model starts with team-strength ratings and adjusts them after verified match results. Expected outcomes depend on the relative ratings and home advantage; results that outperform expectations increase a club’s rating, while disappointing results decrease it. The published Elo gap also feeds the match-probability calculator. Exact update weights and any additional calibration should be taken from the model’s verified methodology, not assumed from the rankings alone.
