Important distinction

The Florida Legislature redrew congressional boundaries for 2026. Historical precinct votes have not yet been assigned to those new districts. A precinct may overlap multiple 2026 districts; neither whole-county nor whole-precinct shortcuts are allowed.

The official precinct archive differs from the certified canvass by one Trump vote in Hardee County. The disagreement is preserved, not concealed or arbitrarily corrected.

PROVENANCE & QUALITY CONTROLS

Nothing guessed. Nothing silently repaired.

The underlying Florida government ZIP contains 537,897 source records, including presidential candidates, other contests and administrative fields. We extracted 39,984 presidential candidate-vote observations across 5,712 precinct identifiers. Each county was checked against a separately published certified Florida presidential canvass.

Official precinct source: 2024 Florida General Election precinct results (ZIP). Certified comparison: Florida Division of Elections presidential canvass.

Florida's original precinct-file totals are Trump/Vance 6,110,126 and Harris/Walz 4,683,038. Its separate certified statewide totals are Trump/Vance 6,110,125 and Harris/Walz 4,683,038. This one-vote discrepancy is isolated to Hardee County. No 2026 election probabilities are generated from the records.
