THE EDITOR'S NOTE / ACCOUNTABILITY

Every number has a source.

Our politics edition is editorially independent and does not endorse candidates. Party colors identify forecasts, not endorsements. We distinguish official election results, polling averages, race ratings, and probabilistic forecasts.
