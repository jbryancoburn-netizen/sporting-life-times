HOW THE COLLEGE SOCCER ELO RATINGS WORK

These are independent power ratings calculated by the Universal Sports Rating System (USRS). We do not reproduce NCAA RPI, coaches’ polls, or outside ratings.

Results and historical input

The model replays published final scores from NCAA.com across men's and women's Divisions I, II and III. Our initial model uses 2025 historical results and completed 2026 games. Each school is identified through NCAA scoreboard team identifiers; duplicate games are removed and uncertain interdivision opponents are not silently assigned to the wrong competition.

Rating calculation

Each team begins at 1,500. An expected result is calculated from the difference in team Elo plus a nominal 75-point home advantage. A win scores 1, a draw 0.5, and a loss 0. An update uses K=28 and a modest goal-margin multiplier, capped at 1.75. A returning program retains 75 percent of its previous season's rating deviation from 1,500.

Reading the rankings

A higher Elo indicates stronger estimated performance inside its own pool. Men's DIII and women's DI ratings are not numerically comparable. The W-L-T and goals columns use accepted NCAA scoreboard games and may differ from official team records while source verification is incomplete.

Matchup-specific forecasts · model v2

Forecasts use a separately calibrated shadow Elo model. Draw chances vary with estimated scoring rates and the strength balance between the two teams. Scoring rates use prior-day team goals for and against, pooled toward each division's average with an eight-game prior. They are not shot-based expected-goals statistics.

A fitted draw model combines Poisson draw log-odds with absolute Elo log-odds. Coefficients were fitted on 8,308 games before October 1, 2026. Men's Division II selected a zero scoring-rate coefficient; its draw chances vary with strength balance. The remaining probability is split between the teams using calibrated shadow Elo. Forecast ratings use K=40, home advantage of 75 points for men's Division I and 50 elsewhere, and full offseason carryover selected on 2025 data. The standings above retain their original provisional rating model.

Important limitations

These rankings are provisional. The college-specific parameters have not been fully calibrated. Neutral-site identification, team membership and result completeness still need independent audit, especially around tournament matches. An NCAA scoreboard listing a nominal home school does not establish that a game was actually played on that campus.

All live forecasts and any betting-oriented predictions require separate calibration and monitoring. A good retrospective result does not guarantee future performance.
