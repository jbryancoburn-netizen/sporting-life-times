THE HIGH-DEFINITION STANDARD

Actual source art must be inspected at intrinsic resolution. Prefer verified 500px-or-better PNG or vector originals, never upscale, keep transparency, preserve aspect ratio and check both a 120px team badge and a 48px scorebug. A logo that has not passed a visual audit stays on the original Victorian mark rather than being described as 1980s verified.

Important: These are draft galleries and an artwork-selection registry. The authentic 1980s college archive is still being sourced; this does not claim that 365 historical college logos have already been obtained or quality-verified. The NBA archive is candidate art until individual files are visually checked and hosted reliably.
