Meeting-by-meeting

Scope: 1985–2019 regular-season, NCAA Tournament, NIT and other secondary-postseason results from historical NCAA archives, plus 2020–2026 recorded games from SportsDataverse NCAA schedules. These are recorded meetings, not a claim of complete postseason coverage, especially for 2020–2026 where most tournament classifications remain under verification. Verified 2021 postseason results and matched 2022–2026 NCAA Tournament results are explicitly labeled. Other recent games may have unverified competition types; an unlabeled postseason game must never be mistaken for a verified regular-season game. Team names follow source labels. Historical game records, head-to-head comparisons, and current Elo ratings are independent products.
