The proposal

This is an editorial model for a stand-alone Group of 6 championship. It is not an announced NCAA or CFP competition. The live illustration uses the current season’s data, rather than inventing tournament results.

  1. Access: reserve one automatic berth for each of the six conference champions. Fill six at-large places from the remaining teams in those conferences. Independents and Power Four programs are outside this proposal.
  2. Selection: this illustration uses independent RETRO ratings for at-large selection and seeding. A final championship would need published eligibility, selection and conference qualification rules. The illustrative field does not certify transition or postseason eligibility.
  3. Seeding: seed all twelve qualifiers by RETRO. Seeds 1–4 receive byes. First round: 5 hosts 12, 6 hosts 11, 7 hosts 10, 8 hosts 9.
  4. The path: quarterfinals pair 1 with the 8/9 winner, 4 with 5/12, 2 with 7/10 and 3 with 6/11. Higher seeds host quarterfinals. The 1/8/9 and 4/5/12 paths meet in one semifinal; the other two paths meet in the other.
  5. The finish: neutral-site semifinals and championship. Dates, venues, revenue arrangements and bowl partnerships remain unspecified.
  6. Relationship to the CFP: this page models a parallel alternative and leaves the existing CFP and bowl projections intact. It currently includes G6 teams even if the separate CFP model selects them. A real adoption would need an explicit rule for CFP participation and replacement berths.

Background: Official 2026–27 CFP format announcement. Team membership and conference records: ESPN standings. Independent ratings: completed 2026 game results, baseline 1500.
