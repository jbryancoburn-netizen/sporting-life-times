ACC · Eighties Logo Archive

Authentic vintage marks for all 18 current ACC schools, with character and mascot artwork preferred whenever documented in the 1980s.

18 archival emblems · Stored locally · For visual approval

This is a proposed audit gallery, not yet the production logo set. Every emblem is a local 512-pixel PNG with two visible school-color rings. Check each mascot's historical character, size, and centering. Source links document which era each design belongs to.

Archival selection standard. A period-correct animal or character mascot takes precedence over a monogram. Where no verifiable 1980s mascot exists, the historical mark is used rather than inventing or backdating modern artwork. This page remains a review gallery; men's and women's production logos will not change until the collection is approved.
