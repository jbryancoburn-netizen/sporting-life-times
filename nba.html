THE ELO TABLES · ALL 30 CLUBS

Three full ranking lists, side by side: current Elo, opening Elo and last season’s final Elo. Current-season Elo remains the authoritative power ranking.

How NBA Elo is calculated Each team begins from its previous season’s final rating, with 60% of its distance from 1,500 carried into the new season. Before each game, the model estimates the home team’s win probability from the rating difference plus a 45-point home advantage. After a final result, both teams gain or lose equal Elo points according to the difference between actual and expected outcome, using K = 20 and a logarithmic margin-of-victory multiplier (weight 0.4). No live, injury or roster adjustments are included in these traditional ratings.
