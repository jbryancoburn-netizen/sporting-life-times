How to read this register

Opponent strength is the arithmetic mean of opponents' current published RETRO ratings across verified final Division I games, not their historical pregame ratings. Best win and worst loss identify the strongest opponent beaten and weakest opponent lost to by that same current-rating standard. These descriptive comparisons do not change the RETRO rating model. Opponent rankings and quality-win thresholds also use current RETRO standings. Missing records remain unreported rather than estimated.
