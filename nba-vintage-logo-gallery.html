HOW TO PICK — Look at each actual historical logo below. Reply with the team and its option letter, for example “Boston A, Denver A, Lakers A.” You can choose different styles team by team. Every option links to the original archival image. This is a research preview only: no choice changes the live site. This is the browser-hosted gallery. If an image is missing, tap “View original image” beneath its card or the team archive link. Images are hosted by the historical-logo source and require internet access.

Historical accuracy: Earlier cities and names are labeled. Seattle SuperSonics marks appear under Oklahoma City as predecessor history, not as vintage Thunder logos. The Bulls have one long-running primary mark, so there is no fabricated alternative. The Raptors, Grizzlies and Pelicans began in later eras.
